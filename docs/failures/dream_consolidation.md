# Dream Consolidation (G15/G18) — What We Simplified

## The shell
G15: offline replay of stored patterns during "dream state". G18: two-
phase dream (NREM/REM analogue). Patterns are replayed by injecting
their vibration signatures back into the substrate. Blending occurs
when two patterns are replayed close in time.

## What a non-thin version requires
- **Hippocampal-cortical dialogue**: real memory consolidation involves
  hippocampal sharp-wave ripples coordinated with cortical slow
  oscillations. Our replay is a simple pattern injection with no
  oscillatory coordination.
- **Synaptic homeostasis hypothesis** (Tononi & Cirelli): sleep
  downscales synaptic weights globally, restoring signal-to-noise. We
  have homeostatic threshold drift (BET-077c) but no global weight
  renormalization during dream.
- **Temporal compression**: hippocampal replay runs 5-20x faster than
  real-time. Our replay runs at simulation speed with no compression.
- **Selective consolidation**: the brain consolidates emotionally
  salient or schema-consistent memories preferentially. Our replay
  is random (or round-robin), with no selection criterion.

## What breaks if wrong
Without proper consolidation dynamics, the substrate cannot build
long-term stable representations. Patterns injected during dream may
interfere destructively instead of consolidating. BET-080 showed 12h
continuous learning — but all training was online, no sleep. Whether
dream consolidation would improve or degrade the learned
representations is unknown.

## When to revisit
When the substrate has multiple distinct learned patterns and we need
them to coexist without catastrophic interference. That's the point
where consolidation becomes necessary rather than optional.
