> Matter joins into **collections**. Each collection develops a **talent**

**Dual regional free-vibration injection** into one world, then measure  
structural specialisation and/or probe-selective response, without pretrained encoders.

## 3. What is established (positive / boundary)

### 3.1 Can form two collections
With sufficient density/time, both spatial halves can hold level≥4 matter (C1b B4=1.0; C4 B4=1.0).  
**Collection existence** under dual regional drive is possible.

### 3.2 Weak structural bias exists
Frequency dual drive produces a **majority** L-low / R-high decade effect (~0.78 trials in C1b) but **fails** the pre-registered 0.90 bar and fails effect-size+control design (C3).  
So: **hint of structure**, not defensible talent.

### 3.3 Probe “talent” is not defensible under injection designs
C2 and C4: controls too high and/or asymmetric artifacts.  
Δ(level≥1 counts) under global or matched probes is a **bad instrument** and/or the substrate does not retain drive-specific response.

### 3.4 Mechanism class CLOSED
**Dual regional free-vibration injection** (different **frequency** *or* different **period**)  
as a path to collection talent is **closed** for this substrate generation.

**Forbidden without a new amendment class:** C5/C6 changing only P_L/P_R, bands, or thresholds.
