# JEP-102 — learning through dialogue: identify the gap, get taught, then know (all three verbs fused)

## Why
The fusion of learn + understand + communicate: when the engine can't answer, it identifies the PRECISE missing
link (its knowledge frontier), can be taught exactly that, and then answers — like a student asking the right
question. Enabled by three-valued comprehension (JEP-101).

## Prediction (locked BEFORE run) [predict-calibrate]
- 🔮 100%: before teaching, inquire() names the frontier ("I know a poodle is an animal, but I don't know whether
  an animal is a living thing"); after telling that one link, assess=yes and explain gives the full chain. MOST-
  LIKELY MISS: frontier when the gap's child IS the frontier, or a/an in the message (handled). Predict 100%.

## Acceptance
- PASS: the inquire->teach->answer loop = 100%. Established (closed-world reasoning + incremental learning), named;
  no novelty. HONEST: identifies a SINGLE missing link on the direct chain; multi-gap / cross-branch teaching is a
  later tier.
