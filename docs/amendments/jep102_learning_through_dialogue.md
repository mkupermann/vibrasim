# JEP-102 — learning through dialogue: identify the gap, get taught, then know (all three verbs fused)

## Why
The fusion of learn + understand + communicate: when the engine can't answer, it identifies the PRECISE missing
link (its knowledge frontier), can be taught exactly that, and then answers — like a student asking the right
question. Enabled by three-valued comprehension (JEP-101).
