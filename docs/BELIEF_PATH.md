## 2. The belief (spine)

In plain language — the story we return to:

1. **Vibrations** move in a shared space.
2. Together they form a **field of energy** (even if the exact rule is not yet known).
3. At a point, energy **binds** → **electrons**.
4. Electrons find partners → **atoms**.
5. Atoms compose → **molecules**.
6. **Molecules carry information** — structure that is not only mass/shape but content.
7. Molecules and atoms form **matter**.
8. Matter joins into **collections**.
9. Each collection develops a **talent** (e.g. light, sound, understanding) from shared physics + different history/drive — not from a separate neural-net stack per sense.
10. Collections of such matter (neuron-like assemblies) compose a **brain**.

End goal (same as CONCEPT §2): every cognitive property is reducible one level down, all the way to vibrations. No transformer, no pretrained embedding, no BPE tokenizer *on this path*. Ports may be engineered (CONCEPT §4.8); **internals must emerge**.

You do not need a science degree to hold this belief. You need locked bars and the courage to accept NULL when the substrate refuses the next rung.

## 5. In bounds / out of bounds

### In bounds (belief path)

- Single substrate (`world/physics`, binding, ambient field, bridges).
- STDP / BTSP / dream only **if** implemented as consequences of matter/molecule dynamics on this substrate — not as a separate weight matrix that replaces molecules.
- Engineered **ports** (where light/sound enter) are allowed; engineered **talents** (a pretrained cochlea model that *is* understanding) are not.
- New or simpler **local rules** that replace parts of the hand table — if pre-registered and compared to the current rule as baseline.
- Falsification of a rung is a success of the process.

### Out of bounds (drift detectors)

- “Just use a reservoir / VSA / LLM for understanding, call it substrate.”  
- Winning a task by abandoning reduction to vibrations.  
- Post-hoc threshold tuning.  
- Re-deriving G30–G153 / memory activity NULLs without a new representation or rule.  
- Dressing known methods as novel emergence.

1. **One rung at a time.** No multi-year fantasy plan as the next commit.
2. **Pre-register** in `docs/amendments/<name>.md` (create the folder if missing) **before** any run: hypothesis, bars, negative controls, time budget (estimate + 2× hard ceiling).

## 7. Open rungs — falsifiable claims (choose one to run next)

Do not start all three. Pick **one**. Bars below are *programme-level sketches*; the live experiment file must lock numbers before data.

### Rung A — Field → bind (bottom)

**Claim:** Free vibrations sustain a measurable energy/density field; binding rate rises when local field exceeds threshold *E\**, not merely when two particles collide under the static 8% table alone.

**Negative controls:** position-shuffled equal density; phase-scrambled / frequency-scrambled drive with matched total energy.

**NULL means:** binding is fully explained by the existing local pair rule; “field” is decorative naming.

### Rung B — Molecules carry information (middle) · **recommended first climb**

**Claim:** Two molecule (or higher-node) species with **different internal structure**, same broad location class, remain decodable as distinct content after a hold interval — and matched controls (scrambled internals, no-structure mass-matched blobs) fall to chance.

**Why first:** The belief says information lives in molecular matter. Matter-*position* memory already works (scoped). This rung asks whether **structure of the bound object**, not only *where* it sits, is a content channel — the missing middle between “atoms exist” and “collections have talent.”

**Negative controls:** scrambled bond/frequency identity; empty cell; stimulus-off.

**NULL means:** under current primitives, molecules are structural foam, not information carriers; talent/brain rungs cannot honestly claim molecule-level content without new primitives.

### Rung C — Collections get talent (upper middle)

**Claim:** One world, one rule set, two drive regions (e.g. optical-like vs acoustic-like *energy injection only* — no separate pretrained feature nets as the talent). After long evolution, the two collections differ in response selectivity above chance; a third unstimulated collection does not.

**Negative controls:** both regions same drive; drive off; labels shuffled post-hoc.

**NULL means:** specialization does not emerge; talents require engineered modules (charter-honest ports only, or a redesign of primitives).
