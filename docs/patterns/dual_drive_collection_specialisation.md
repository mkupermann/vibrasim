# Pattern: dual-drive collection specialisation (from BP-C1 / C1b)

## Claim class
Two spatial regions + different free-vibration frequency drives (no ML sense nets)
→ local matter (level≥4 frequency decades) can specialise by region.

## What worked
- **Density first:** C1 NULLed on population (B4); C1b denser/longer regime → B4=1.00.
- **Controls:** same-band and flipped-inequality stay low when dual-drive is on.
- **Majority effect:** C1b achieved **0.778** specialisation (7/9) — real signal.

## What failed the bar
- Locked ≥0.90 was not met. Noise/seed variance still flips ~1/4 of trials.
- Do **not** retune 0.90 post-hoc; open a new amendment if a different claim is wanted
  (e.g. effect-size test, or probe-response talent BP-C2).
